# Harbor & Co. Field Guide

A pocket guide for analysts who read client documents and have to show their work.

## Refund policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days. Expedited shipping fees are non-refundable. After 30 days, store credit may be offered at the manager's discretion.

## Reading room hours

The reading room is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. The north archive is closed on Wednesdays for conservation work. Visitors sign the paper log before entering the stacks.

## How citations should look

Analysts cite the page and a short snippet. Every claim in a client memo must point back to a source passage. If the document does not contain the answer, say so plainly instead of guessing.

## Retention

Source files are kept for 18 months and then deleted unless a matter is on hold. The client can request deletion sooner from the records desk.
